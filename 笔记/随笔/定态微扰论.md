## 11.1

求非简并能级的三阶微扰修正公式

>[!S]
>设 $H=H_{0}+H'$ 其中 $H'$ 为微扰，而本征态和本征函数可以表示为
>$$\begin{gather} \psi_{n} = \psi_{n,0} + \psi_{n,1} + \psi_{n,2} + \dots \\ E_{n} = E_{n,0} + E_{n,1} + E_{n,2} + \dots \end{gather} $$
>其中满足
>$$\begin{gather} H_{0}\psi_{n,0} = E_{n,0}\psi_{n,0} \\ \braket{ \psi_{n,0} | \psi_{n,s} } = 0.\quad s=1,2,\dots  \end{gather} $$
>则
>$$H\psi_{n} = (H_{0}+H')\psi_{n} = E_{n}\psi_{n} $$
>按阶展开得到，下略 $n$ 
>$$\begin{align}  & (H_{0}-E_{0})\psi_{0} = 0 \\ & (H_{0}-E_{0})\psi_{1} = (E_{1}-H')\psi_{0} \\ & (H_{0}-E_{0})\psi_{2} = (E_{1}-H')\psi_{1} + E_{2}\psi_{0} \\ & (H_{0}-E_{0})\psi_{3} = (E_{1}-H')\psi_{2} + E_{2}\psi_{1} + E_{3}\psi_{0} \end{align} $$
>其中 $\psi_{0},E_{0}$ 是已知的，我们想要表示出来 $E_{1},E_{2},E_{3}$ 由第二个显然
>$$\braket{ \psi_{0} |(H_{0}-E_{0})| \psi_{1} } = 0 = E_{1} - \braket{ \psi_{0} |H'| \psi_{0} } \implies E_{1} = \braket{ \psi_{0} |H'|\psi_{0}  }    $$
>同理
>$$\begin{gather} E_{2} = \braket{ \psi_{0} |H'| \psi_{1} } ,\quad E_{3} = \braket{ \psi_{0} |H'| \psi_{2} }   \end{gather} $$
>接下来以 $\psi_{1},\psi_{2}$ 与上面方程做内积
>$$\braket{ \psi_{1} |(H_{0}-E_{0})| \psi_{2} } = \braket{ \psi_{1} |(E_{1}-H')| \psi_{1} } = E_{1} - \braket{ \psi_{1} | H'| \psi_{1} }   $$
>$$\braket{ \psi_{2} |(H_{0}-E_{0})| \psi_{1} } = -\braket{ \psi_{2} |H'| \psi_{0} } = - E_{3}  $$
>由于 $H_{0}-E_{0}$ 是厄米的，所以 $\braket{ \psi_{1} | (H_{0}-E_{0})|\psi_{2} }$ 是实数，上下两个值相等
>$$E_{3} = \braket{ \psi_{1} | H'|\psi_{1} } - E_{1}\braket{ \psi_{1} | \psi_{1} }  $$
>而
>$$\psi_{n,1} = \sum_{k\neq n} \frac{H_{kn}'}{E_{n,0}-E_{k,0}}\psi_{k,0} $$
>故
>$$\begin{align}\braket{ \psi_{1} | H'|\psi_{1} } & = \sum_{k\neq n}\braket{ \psi_{n,1} | H'| \psi_{k,0} } \frac{H'_{kn}}{E_{n,0}-E_{k,0}} \\ &  = \sum_{k,l\neq n} \frac{H_{kn}'H_{\ln}'}{(E_{n,0}-E_{k,0})(E_{n,0}-E_{l,0})} \braket{ \psi_{l,0} | H'| \psi_{k,0} } \\  & = \sum_{k,l\neq n} \frac{H_{kn}'H_{\ln}'H_{lk}'}{(E_{n,0}-E_{k,0})(E_{n,0}-E_{l,0})}\end{align} $$
>$$\begin{align} \braket{ \psi_{1} | \psi_{1} } = \sum_{k,l\neq n} \frac{H_{kn}'H_{\ln}'}{(E_{n,0}-E_{k,0})(E_{n,0}-E_{l,0})} \braket{ \psi_{l,0} | \psi_{k,0} } = \sum_{k\neq n} \frac{|H_{kn}'|^{2}}{{(E_{n,0}-E_{k,0})^{2}}} \end{align} $$
>得到
>$$E_{3} = \sum_{k,l\neq n} \frac{H_{kn}'H_{\ln}'H_{lk}'}{(E_{n,0}-E_{k,0})(E_{n,0}-E_{l,0})} - H_{nn}'\sum_{k\neq n} \frac{|H_{kn}'|^{2}}{{(E_{n,0}-E_{k,0})^{2}}} $$

## 11.2

有一个量子力学系统，其哈密顿量为
$$H = H_{0} + H' $$
其中 $H_{0}$ 为零级，$H'$ 为微扰，$H_{0}$ 的本征态和本征值是
$$\psi_{n}^{(0)} ,\quad E_{n}^{(0)} $$
设能级 $E_{n}^{(0)}$ 是二重简并的，相应的记正交归一的本征态是 $\psi_{n\alpha}^{(0)},\psi_{n\beta}^{(0)}$ 简记为 $\psi_{\alpha},\psi_{\beta}$ 在 $H_{0}$ 表象中，微扰矩阵元记为
$$\begin{align}
 & \braket{ \psi_{n\alpha}^{(0)} | H'|\psi_{k}^{(0)} } = H'_{\alpha k} \\
  & \braket{ \psi_{n\beta}^{(0)} | H'|\psi_{k}^{(0)} } =H'_{\beta k} \\
   & \braket{ \psi_{n\alpha}^{(0)} | H'|\psi_{n\beta}^{(0)} } =H_{\alpha \beta}' \\
    & \braket{ \psi_{n\alpha}^{(0)} | H'|\psi_{n\alpha}^{(0)} } =\varepsilon_{\alpha} \\
	 & \braket{ \psi_{n\beta}^{(0)} | H'|\psi_{n\beta}^{(0)} } =\varepsilon_{\beta}
\end{align} $$
试求出 $E_{n}$ 的二阶微扰修正


>[]
>在零阶微扰简并空间本征态下，微扰矩阵表达
>$$H'=\begin{pmatrix} \varepsilon_{a} & H_{ab} \\ H_{ba} & \varepsilon_{b} \end{pmatrix} $$
>现在本征方程为
>$$(H_{0}+H')\ket{\psi} = E\ket{\psi} \implies \begin{pmatrix} E_{n}^{(0)} + \varepsilon_{a} - E  & H_{ab} \\ H_{ba} & E_{n}^{(0)}+\varepsilon_{b}-E  \end{pmatrix}\begin{pmatrix} \ket{\alpha} \\ \ket{\beta}  \end{pmatrix} = 0 $$
>久期方程有
>$$\det\begin{pmatrix} E_{n}^{(0)} + \varepsilon_{a} - E  & H_{ab} \\ H_{ba} & E_{n}^{(0)}+\varepsilon_{b}-E  \end{pmatrix} = 0  $$
>$$E_{\pm}^{(1)} = \frac{\varepsilon_{a}+\varepsilon_{b}\pm \sqrt{ (\varepsilon_{a}-\varepsilon_{b})^{2} - 4|H_{ab}|^{2} }}{2} $$
>对应的
>$$\ket{\psi}  $$

## 11.4

粒子在一维势场 $V(x)$ 中运动，能级为 $E_{n}^{(0)}$ 如收到微扰 $H'=\frac{\lambda}{m}p$ 作用，求能级的三阶修正

>[!S]
>记 $P_{n}=\ket{n^{(0)}} \bra{n^{(0)}},Q_{n}=1-P_{n},R_{n}=\frac{Q_{n}}{E_{n}^{(0)}-H_{0}}$ 则
>$$E_{n}^{(1)} = \braket{ n^{(0)} |V| n^{(0)} } = \frac{1}{m}\braket{ n^{(0)} | p|n^{(0)} } =0 $$
>$$\ket{n^{(1)}} = \frac{Q_{n}}{E_{n}^{(0)}-H_{0}}Q_{n}V\ket{n^{(0)}} =R_{n}V\ket{n^{(0)}} - R_{n}\ket{n^{(0)}}\braket{ n^{(0)} |V| n^{(0)} } = R_{n}V\ket{n^{(0)}}  $$
>$$E_{n}^{(2)} = \braket{ n^{(0)} | V|n^{(1)} } = \braket{ n^{(0)} | VR_{n}V|n^{(0)} } = \sum_{m\neq n} \frac{\braket{ n^{(0)} | V|m^{(0)} }\braket{ m^{(0)} | V|n^{(0)} }}{E_{n}^{(0)}-E_{m}^{(0)}} = \frac{1}{2m} $$
>$$\begin{align} \ket{n^{(2)}}  & = -\frac{Q_{n}}{E_{n}^{(0)}-H_{0}}[ E_{n}^{(1)}\ket{n^{(1)}} - Q_{n}V\ket{n^{(1)}}  ] \\ & = -E_{n}^{(1)}R_{n}^{2} V\ket{n^{(0)}} + R_{n}Q_{n}VR_{n}V\ket{n^{(0)}} \end{align} $$
>所以
>$$\begin{align} E_{n}^{(3)} &  = \braket{ n^{(0)} |V| n^{(2)} } \\ & = -E_{n}^{(1)}\braket{ n^{(0)} |VR_{n}^{2}V| n^{(0)} } + \braket{ n^{(0)} |R_{n}Q_{n}VR_{n}V| n^{(0)} } \end{align} $$
>其中代入 $R_{n}$ 的定义
>$$\begin{align} \braket{ n^{(0)} |VR_{n}^{2}V n^{(0)} } & = \sum_{k,l\neq n} \frac{\braket{ n^{(0)} | V| k^{(0)} }\braket{ k^{(0)} | l^{(0)} }\braket{ l^{(0)} | V|n^{(0)} }}{(E_{n}^{(0)}-E_{k}^{(0)})(E_{n}^{(0)}-E_{l}^{(0)})} \\ & = \sum_{k\neq n} \frac{\braket{ n^{(0)} | V|k^{(0)} }\braket{ k^{(0)} | V|n^{(0)} }}{(E_{n}^{(0)}-E_{k}^{(0)})^{2}} \\ & = \sum_{k\neq n} \frac{|\braket{ n^{(0)} | p|k^{(0)} }|^{2}}{m(E_{n}^{(0)}-E_{k}^{(0)})^{2}} \\ & = 0  \end{align}  $$
>$$\begin{align} \braket{ n^{(0)} | R_{n}Q_{n}VR_{n}V| n^{(0)} }  & = \sum_{k,l,m\neq n} \frac{\braket{ n^{(0)} | k^{(0)} }\braket{ k^{(0)} | m^{(0)} }\braket{ m^{(0)} | V|l^{(0)} }\braket{ l^{(0)} | V|n^{(0)} }}{(E_{n}^{(0)}-E_{k}^{(0)})(E_{n}^{(0)}-E_{l}^{(0)})} \\ & = \sum_{k,l\neq n} \frac{\braket{ n^{(0)} | k^{(0)} }\braket{ k^{0} | V|l^{(0)} }\braket{ l^{(0)} | V|n^{(0)} }}{(E_{n}^{(0)}-E_{k}^{(0)})(E_{n}^{(0)}-E_{l}^{(0)})} \\ & = 0 \end{align} $$
>即 $E_{n}^{(3)}=0$ 
>

实际上我们注意到
$$[R_{n},V] = \frac{1}{m} [R_{n},p] = \frac{1}{m}\sum_{k\neq n} \frac{1}{E_{n}^{(0)}-E_{k}^{(0)}}(\ket{k^{(0)}} \bra{k^{(0)}}p - p\ket{k^{(0)}} \bra{k^{(0)}}) = 0 $$
故 $V$ 在 $H_{0}$ 的本征子空间上满足选择定则。此外 $V$ 的生成元 $p$ 是 $H_{0}$ 的 Casimir 算符，理论上可以精确求解，事实也正是如此，本题中在加上微扰 $V=\frac{\lambda p}{m}$ 后的本征能量就是 $E_{n}=E_{n}^{(0)}-\frac{\lambda^{2}}{2m}$
