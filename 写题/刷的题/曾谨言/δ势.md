# 坐标表象
## 2.1

粒子在 $\delta$ 势阱
$$V(x) = -V_{0}\delta(x) $$
中运动，求束缚态能级和波函数

>[!S]
>定态薛定谔方程是
>$$\psi''(x) + \frac{2m}{\hbar}[E+V_{0}\delta(x)]\psi(x) = 0 $$
>当 $E<0$ 时为束缚态，对这个式子在 $-\varepsilon \sim\varepsilon$ 附近积分得到
>$$\int_{-\varepsilon}^{+\varepsilon} \psi''dx = \psi'(\varepsilon) - \psi'(-\varepsilon) = \frac{2m}{\hbar^{2}}(2\varepsilon E + V_{0})\psi(0) = \frac{2m}{\hbar^{2}}\int_{-\varepsilon}^{+\varepsilon}(E+V_{0}\delta(x))\psi dx $$
>令 $\varepsilon\to 0$ 得到
>$$\psi'(0^{+}) - \psi'(0^{-}) = -\frac{2mV_{0}}{\hbar^{2}} \psi(0) \tag{1}$$
>令
>$$k = \frac{\sqrt{ -2mE }}{\hbar} ,\quad \beta = \frac{\sqrt{ -2mV_{0} }}{\hbar} $$
>则
>$$\psi'' - k^{2}\psi = 0 \implies \psi(x) \sim e^{ -k|x| } $$
>对于偶宇称
>$$\psi(x) = \begin{cases} Ce^{ -kx }, & x>0 \\Ce^{ kx }, & x<0 \end{cases} $$
>考虑到 (1) 得到
>$$-2Ck = -\frac{2mV_{0}C}{\hbar^{2}} \implies E = -\frac{mV_{0}^{2}}{2\hbar} $$
>由归一化条件得到 $C = \sqrt{ k }$ 
>对于奇宇称
>$$\psi(x)=\begin{cases}Ae^{ -kx }, & x>0 \\ -Ae^{ kx }, & x<0\end{cases} $$
>此时 $\psi(0)=0\implies A=0$ 没有束缚态


## 2.3

求动能为 $E$ 的粒子对 $\delta$ 势垒
$$V(x) = V_{0}\delta(x) $$
的透射系数

>[!S]
>定态薛定谔方程为
>$$\psi'' + \frac{2m}{\hbar^{2}}(E-V_{0}\delta(x))\psi = 0 $$
>在 $x=0$ 处导数跳跃 $\psi'(0^{+})-\psi'(0^{-})=\frac{2mV_{0}}{\hbar^{2}}\psi(0)$ 令 $k = \frac{\sqrt{ 2mE }}{\hbar} ,\quad \beta = \frac{\sqrt{ 2mV_{0} }}{\hbar}$ 则
>$$\psi'' + k^{2}\psi = 0,\quad \psi'(0^{+}) - \psi'(0^{-}) = \beta^{2}\psi(0) $$
>设
>$$\psi(x) = \begin{cases} e^{ ikx } + R e^{ -ikx } , & x<0 \\ Te^{ ikx }, & x>0 \end{cases} $$
>于是
>$$1+R = T ,\quad ik(R + T - 1) = \beta^{2}(1+R) $$
>解得
>$$T = \frac{\beta^{2}}{ik-\beta^{2}} ,\quad R = \frac{ik}{ik - \beta^{2}} $$


## 2.7

粒子在势场
$$V(x) = \begin{cases}
V_{0}\delta(x), & |x|<a \\
\infty, & |x|>a
\end{cases} $$
中运动，求能级公式，将其展开到关于 $V_{0}$ 的最小非平凡阶

>[!S]
>设 $k=\frac{\sqrt{ 2mE }}{\hbar},\beta=\frac{\sqrt{ 2mV_{0} }}{\hbar}$ 则
>$$\psi'' + k^{2}\psi =0 ,\quad \psi'(0^{+})-\psi(0^{-}) = \beta^{2}\psi(0) $$
>于是特解为 $\sin kx,\cos kx$ 对于奇宇称由于 $\psi(0)=0$ 所以 $\psi'(0^{+})=\psi'(0^{-})$ 故
>$$\psi(x) = A\sin kx ,\quad -a<x<a $$
>由边界条件 $\psi(a)=0$ 知 $k=\frac{n\pi}{a}$ 能级为
>$$E = \frac{\hbar^{2}k^{2}}{2m} = \frac{n^{2}\pi^{2}\hbar^{2}}{2ma^{2}} $$
>对于偶宇称
>$$\psi(x)=\begin{cases} A\cos kx + B\sin kx, & 0<x<a \\ A\cos kx - B\sin kx, & -a<x<0 \end{cases} $$
>于是
>$$A\cos ka + B\sin ka = 0 ,\quad 2Bk = A\beta^{2} $$
>解得
>$$\tan x = -\frac{2}{\beta^{2}a}x ,\quad x=ka $$
>对于该方程在 $\beta\to 0$ 时 $\tan x\approx 0$ 故 $x^{0}=\left( n+\frac{1}{2} \right)\pi$ 设 $x=\left( n+\frac{1}{2} \right)\pi + \delta$ 则
>$$\tan x = \tan\left( \frac{\pi}{2} + \delta \right) = -\cot\delta = \frac{1}{\delta} + \mathcal{O}(\delta) = -\frac{2}{\beta^{2}a}\left[ \left( n+\frac{1}{2} \right)\pi+\delta \right] $$
>得
>$$\delta = \frac{\beta^{2}a}{(2n+1)\pi} $$
>$$E = \frac{\hbar^{2}k^{2}}{2m} = \frac{\hbar^{2}}{2m}\left[  \left( n+\frac{1}{2} \right)\pi + \delta  \right]^{2} = \frac{1}{2m}\left[ \left( n+\frac{1}{2} \right)n\hbar \right] + \frac{V_{0}}{a} $$

# 动量表象

## 9.4

在动量表象中解 $\delta$ 势阱
$$V(x) = -V_{0}\delta(x) $$
的束缚态能级和本征函数

>[!S]
>定态薛定谔方程为
>$$\psi''(x) - k^{2}\psi + \beta^{2}\delta(x)\psi = 0 ,\quad k=\sqrt{ -2mE },\beta=\sqrt{ 2mV_{0} } $$
>傅里叶变换
>$$\psi(x) = \int \tilde{\psi}(p)e^{ -ipx }dp ,\quad \tilde{\psi}(p) = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int \psi(x)e^{ ipx }dx $$
>于是
>$$(k^{2}+p^{2})\tilde{\psi}(p) = \frac{\beta^{2}}{2\pi}\psi(0)$$
>所以
>$$\tilde{\psi}(p) = \frac{\beta^{2}}{2\pi}\psi(0) \frac{1}{p^{2}-k^{2}} $$
>$$\psi(x) =  \frac{\beta^{2}}{2\pi}\psi(0) \int \frac{e^{ ipx }}{p^{2}-k^{2}}dp $$
>
>其中
>$$\int \frac{e^{ ipx }}{p^{2}-k^{2}}dp = \begin{cases} \dfrac{\pi}{k}e^{ -kx } , & x>0 \\ \dfrac{\pi}{k}e^{ kx }, & x<0 \end{cases} \quad = \frac{\pi}{k}e^{ -k|x| } $$
>于是
>$$\psi(x) = \frac{\beta^{2}}{2k}\psi(0)e^{ -k|x| } $$
>所以
>$$\frac{\beta^{2}}{2k} = 1 \implies 2\sqrt{ -2mE } = 2mV_{0} \implies E = -\frac{mV_{0}^{2}}{2\hbar^{2}} $$
>通过归一化可得
>$$\psi(x) = \sqrt{ k }e^{ -k|x| } $$
>

$$\begin{align} \braket{ p |\delta(x)| \psi } & = \int \braket{ p | x } \braket{ x |\delta(x)| \psi } dx \\ & = \int \frac{e^{ ipx }}{\sqrt{ 2\pi }} \psi(x) \delta(x) dx \\ & = \frac{\psi(0)}{\sqrt{ 2\pi }}   \end{align} $$

## 9.6

用动量表象计算粒子对于 $\delta$ 势垒 $V(x)=V_{0}\delta(x)$ 的透射率

>[!S]
>定态薛定谔方程为
>$$p^{2}\tilde{\psi} + \beta^{2}\psi(0) = k^{2}\tilde{\psi} ,\quad k=\sqrt{ 2mE },\beta=\sqrt{ 2mV_{0} } $$
>傅里叶变换
>$$\psi(x) = \int \tilde{\psi}(p)e^{ -ipx }dp ,\quad \tilde{\psi}(p) = \frac{1}{2\pi}\int \psi(x)e^{ -ipx }dx $$
>于是
>$$(p^{2} - k^{2})\tilde{\psi}(p) = -\frac{\beta^{2}}{2\pi}\psi(0) $$
>考虑到方程 $(p^{2}-k^{2})\tilde{\psi}(p)=0$ 有解 $\tilde{\psi}(p)=\delta(p-k),\delta(p+k)$ 于是
>$$\begin{align} \psi(x) & = C_{1}e^{ ikx } + C_{2}e^{ -ikx } - \frac{\beta^{2}}{2\pi}\psi(0)\int \frac{e^{ ipx }}{p^{2}-k^{2}}dp \\ & = C_{1}e^{ ikx } + C_{2}e^{ -ikx } - \frac{i\beta^{2}\psi(0)}{2k}e^{ -k|x| } \end{align} \tag{0}$$
>显然 $x=0$ 时有方程
>$$\psi(0) = C_{1} + C_{2} - \frac{i\beta^{2}}{2k}\psi(0) \tag{1} $$
>对于透射波雨反射波的情况，总的波函数应当可以写为如下形式
>$$\begin{gather} \psi(x) = e^{ ikx } + R e^{ -ikx }, & x<0 \\ \psi(x) = Te^{ ikx }, & x>0 \end{gather} $$
>对比 (0) 可知
>$$C_{1} = T ,\quad C_{2} - \frac{i\beta^{2}}{2k}\psi(0) = 0 ,\quad C_{1} - \frac{i\beta^{2}}{2k}\psi(0) = 1 ,\quad C_{2} = R $$
>所以
>$$T = 1 - \frac{i\beta^{2}}{2k}\psi(0) , \quad R = \frac{i\beta^{2}}{2k}\psi(0) $$
>考虑到 (1) 得到 $\psi(0) = \left( 1+\frac{i\beta^{2}}{2k} \right)^{-1}$ 于是
>$$T = \frac{1}{1+\frac{i\beta^{2}}{2k}} ,\quad R = \frac{\frac{i\beta^{2}}{2k}}{1 + \frac{i\beta^{2}}{2k}} $$


## 9.7

质量为 $m$ 的粒子束以动量 $p=\hbar k$ 从 $x=-\infty$ 处入射，受到周期性的 $\delta$ 势垒
$$V(x) = V_{0} \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \delta(x-na) $$
的作用，求能够完全反射的动量值

>[!S]
>类似的 $k=\sqrt{ 2mE },\beta=\sqrt{ 2mV_{0} }$ 由薛定谔方程得到
>$$p^{2}\tilde{\psi}(p) - \frac{\beta^{2}}{2\pi}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\psi(na) = k^{2}\tilde{\psi}(p) $$
>于是
>$$\tilde{\psi}(p) = C_{1}\delta(p-k) + C_{2}\delta(p+k) + \frac{\beta^{2}}{2\pi}\sum \psi(na) \frac{1}{p^{2}-k^{2}}$$
>$$\psi(x) = C_{1}e^{ ikx } + C_{2}e^{ -ikx } + \frac{i\beta^{2}}{2k}\sum \psi(na) e^{ -ik|x| }  $$
>而物理上应该有
>$$\begin{gather} \psi(x) = e^{ ikx } + R e^{ -ikx } , & x<-0 \\ \psi(x) = Te^{ ikx }, & x>+\infty \end{gather} $$
>于是
>$$1+ \frac{i\beta^{2}}{2k}\sum \psi(na)e^{ -ikna } = 0$$
>
>代入 $x=ma$ 得到
>$$\psi(ma) = e^{ ikma } + C_{2}e^{ -ikma } + \frac{i\beta^{2}}{2\pi} \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \psi(na)e^{ ik|m-n|a } $$
>设 $\psi(x)=Ae^{ iKx }$ 则
>$$\cos Ka = \cos ka + \frac{\beta^{2}}{2\pi}\sin ka $$
>于是无透射波时，有
>$$\left|\cos ka + \frac{\beta^{2}}{2\pi}\sin ka\right| > 1 $$
>对应的
>$$k = \frac{n\pi}{a} \implies p = \frac{n\pi \hbar}{a} $$
