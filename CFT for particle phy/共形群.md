## 生成元


### general
在李群表示论中，生成元是无穷小变换在流形上的微分算子，给定一个无穷小变换坐标变换
$$x^{\mu} \to x^{\mu} + \varepsilon^{\mu}(x) $$
其中 $\varepsilon^{\mu}(x)$ 是无穷小量，我们想要找到对应的微分算子 $X$，使得其对流形上的任意光滑函数有
$$Xf(x) = \delta f(x) = f(x') - f(x) = -\varepsilon^{\mu}\partial_{\mu}f + \mathcal{O}(\varepsilon^{2}) $$
于是
$$X = \varepsilon^{\mu}\partial_{\mu} $$
*数学家喜欢微分算符 $X$ 而物理学家只感兴趣厄米算符 $G=-iX$*

若该李群可以用一个单参数来描述，参数记为 $t$ 则无穷小变换记为
$$x^{\mu} \to x^{\mu} + t\xi^{\mu}(x) + \mathcal{O}(t^{2}) $$
则
$$(T_{t}f)(x) = f(T_{-t}x) = f(x^{\mu} - t\xi^{\mu} + \mathcal{O})(t^{2}) $$
对 $t$ 在 $t=0$ 求导得到

$$\frac{d}{dt}\bigg|_{t=0}f(x) = -\xi^{\mu}\partial_{\mu}f(x) $$
因此生成元是

$$G(x) = -i\xi^{\mu}(x)\partial_{\mu} $$
*如果 $G$ 本身是有指标的，那么它应当在 $\xi^{\mu}$ 上体现出来，也就是说有内部指标*

已知共形 killing 方程的通解是

$$\varepsilon^{\mu} = a^{\mu} + \omega^{\mu}{}_{\nu}x^{\nu} + \lambda x^{\mu} + x^{2}b^{\mu} - 2(b\cdot x)x^{\mu} $$

### $P^{\mu}$
对于参数 $a^{\mu}$，取 $a^{\mu} = \delta^{\mu}_{\nu}a^{\nu}$，对于固定的 $\nu$ 令 $a^{\nu}=t$ 其它分量为零则

$$\xi^{\mu}=1$$
$$P^{\mu} = -i\partial^{\mu} $$
### $M^{\mu \nu}$
对于参数 $\omega^{\mu \nu}$ 取 $\omega^{\mu \nu}=-\omega^{\nu \mu}$ 取指标 $\rho ,\sigma$ 令 $\omega^{\rho \sigma}=\theta$ 其余的分量为零则

$$\omega^{\mu}{}_{\nu} = \theta(\delta^{\mu}_{\rho}\delta_{\sigma \nu} - \delta^{\mu}_{\sigma}\delta_{\rho \nu}) $$
$$\varepsilon^{\mu} = \theta(\delta^{\mu}_{\rho}x_{\sigma} - \delta^{\mu}_{\sigma}x_{\rho}) $$
$$\xi^{\mu} = \delta^{\mu}_{\rho}x_{\sigma} - \delta^{\mu}_{\sigma}x_{\rho} $$
$$M_{\rho \sigma} = -i(x_{\rho}\partial_{\sigma} - x_{\sigma}\partial_{\rho}) $$

### $D$
对于参数 $\lambda$ 有

$$\varepsilon^{\mu}=\lambda x^{\mu} $$
$$\xi^{\mu} = x^{\mu} $$
$$D = -ix^{\mu}\partial_{\mu} $$

### $K^{\mu}$
对于参数 $b^{\mu}$ 有 $\varepsilon^{\mu}=x^{2}b^{\mu}-2(x\cdot b)x^{\mu}$ 是关于 $b^{\mu}$ 的线性函数，可以写为 $b^{\nu}$ 乘上某个系数。固定 $\nu$ 令 $b^{\nu}=t$ 并令其它分量为零，则

$$\varepsilon^{\mu} = t(\delta^{\mu}{}_{\nu}x^{2} - 2\delta^{\nu}{}_{\rho}x^{\rho}x^{\mu}) $$
$$\xi^{\mu} = \delta^{\mu}{}_{\nu}x^{2} - 2x^{\mu}x_{\nu} $$
$$K^{\mu} = -i( x^{2}\partial^{\mu} - 2x^{\mu}x_{\nu}\partial^{\nu} ) $$

## 有限变换

李群李代数间有指数映射的关系，所以对于给定了微分算符 $X$ 以及它对应的参数 $t$ 我们就能唯一的获得对应的李群元素 (变换)

$$U_{X}(t) \equiv \exp(tX) : x^{\mu}\to U_{X}(t)x^{\mu} $$
或者我们用生成元 $G$
$$U_{G}(t)\equiv \exp(\mathrm{i}tG): x^{\mu}\to U_{G}(t)x^{\mu} $$

### 平移
取生成元 $P^{\mu}$ 参数 $a^{\mu}$ 则

$$U_{P}(a^{\mu}) = \exp(ia^{\mu}P_{\mu}) = \exp(ia^{\mu}\partial_{\mu}) $$
$$x^{\mu}\to \exp(ia^{\mu}\partial_{\mu})x^{\mu} = x^{\mu} + a^{\mu} $$

### 旋转/洛伦兹
考虑生成元 $M_{\mu \nu}$ ，参数为 $\theta$ 则

$$U_{M_{\mu \nu}}(\theta) = \exp(i\theta^{\mu \nu}M_{\mu \nu}) = \exp[i\theta^{\mu \nu}(x_{\mu}\partial_{\nu} - x_{\nu}\partial_{\mu})] $$
$$x^{\mu} \to \exp[i\theta^{\mu \nu}(x_{\mu}\partial_{\nu} - x_{\nu}\partial_{\mu})]x^{\mu} = \Lambda^{\mu}{}_{\nu}x^{\nu} $$

### 标度
考虑生成元 $D$，参数为 $\lambda$ 则

$$U_{D}(\lambda) = \exp(i\lambda D) = \exp(i\lambda x^{\mu}\partial_{\mu}) $$
$$x^{\mu} \to \exp(i\lambda x^{\mu}\partial_{\mu})x^{\mu} = e^{ \lambda } x^{\mu} $$

### SCT
考虑生成元 $K^{\mu}$ ，参数为 $b^{\mu}$ 则

$$U_{K^{\mu}}(b^{\mu}) = \exp(ib^{\mu}K_{\mu})=\exp[ib^{\mu}(x^{2}\partial_{\mu} - 2x_{\mu}x_{\nu}\partial^{\nu})] $$
$$x^{\mu}\to \exp(\dots)x^{\mu} = \dots = \frac{x^{\mu}+x^{2}b^{\mu}}{1+2b\cdot x + b^{2}x^{2}} $$
